Climate Debt & Territorial Forest Carbon by Country

Five views of national carbon responsibility and carbon stewardship. Tabs 1–4 are global choropleth maps; tab 5 is India state-level forest carbon stock. Hover any country or state for detailed figures.

Linear time-weight: Σ(annual fossil CO₂Y × (2024−Y+1)). No absorption correction. Units: Gt-CO₂·yr. Conservative worst-case for historical responsibility.
Bern IRF (IPCC AR5, Joos et al. 2013): Σ(annual fossil CO₂Y × IRF(2024−Y)). IRF permanent floor = 0.217 — no emission ever fully erased. Units: Gt-CO₂-equiv. atmospheric burden.
ELUC-adjusted linear debt: Net annual emissions (fossil CO₂ + land-use change CO₂) × years elapsed. Source: OWID/GCB national ELUC series (1850–2024). Deforestation nations (Brazil, Indonesia, USA historically) see substantially higher debt. Afforestation nations (France, South Korea) see minor reductions. Units: Gt-CO₂·yr.
Territorial forest carbon stocks (all pools): Above-ground biomass + below-ground biomass + dead wood + litter + soil organic carbon, by country. Source: FAO Global Forest Resources Assessment 2020 (Main Report Table 3 & country annexes). Converted from MtC using factor 3.664. Units: GtCO₂-equivalent. High-stock countries are providing an ongoing climate service independent of their emissions trajectory.
India subnational forest carbon stocks: Total carbon stock (all pools) by state/UT. Source: Forest Survey of India, India State of Forest Report 2021 (Statement 5) and ISFR 2023 (area). Converted from MtC using factor 3.664. Units: Mt-CO₂-equivalent. Arunachal Pradesh, MP, and Chhattisgarh collectively hold >30% of India’s territorial forest carbon.
